Privacy Policy

Last updated:

The short version

Your photos never leave your device. The conversion tool on this site runs entirely in your browser, so we have no ability to see, store, or transmit the files you convert.

What we don't collect

  • The photos you upload or convert
  • Filenames or metadata (EXIF data) from your photos
  • Any copy of your converted files

What we may collect

Like most websites, we may use privacy-respecting analytics to understand general traffic patterns, such as which pages are visited and which browsers are used. This data is aggregated and is not tied to the photos you convert, since those never reach our servers in the first place.

Cookies

This site does not use cookies to track you across other websites. Any cookies in use are limited to basic functionality, such as remembering a preference you've set.

Third-party services

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and the HEIC decoding library is loaded from a public CDN (jsDelivr). These services may log standard request information such as IP address, as is typical for any resource loaded from the web. Neither service receives your photo files.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact page.
